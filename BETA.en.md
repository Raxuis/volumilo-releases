# Try Volumilo — initial beta

[Overview](README.en.md) · [Français](BETA.fr.md) · [Installation](QUICKSTART.en.md)

An initial wave of **10–15 volunteers** is proposed. You do not need technical expertise. This is a recruitment target, not a count of people already enrolled.

**Before you begin:** Apple Silicon Mac, macOS 14+, development release with local signing and no Apple notarization. Read the [installation guide](QUICKSTART.en.md). Source code is private. No account is needed to download; a GitHub account is needed to send feedback.

## A first try in 10 minutes

Use a small demonstration folder containing disposable copies. Keep the originals and your usual backups. This walkthrough requires no deletion.

1. **Open**: note whether downloading and first launch are understandable. Do not change macOS global security protections.
2. **Explore**: scan the demonstration folder, open a folder in the chart, then go up. Try the Contents list too.
3. **Prepare a selection**: drag one item into Collector. It should appear there without leaving its original folder. Remove it from the selection afterwards. Do not confirm Trash or permanent deletion.
4. **Understand**: find one useful piece of information, then note a label, screen or action that made you hesitate.
5. **Share feedback**: give your Volumilo and macOS versions, what you tried and what happened.

## For your use

- **Everyday Mac use**: try Application Storage on an app you choose. Review the results without removing its caches or data.
- **Development**: explore a disposable copy of a small local project in Developer Projects. Check whether generated-folder explanations make sense. A suggestion does not prove an item is unnecessary.
- **External disk or SD card**: scan an entire small test volume, explicitly save its catalogue, eject it in Finder, then search for a fictitious name. The result should be marked as a past observation. Never share the catalogue itself.
- **Transfer, optional**: if you have another writable external volume, use small fictitious files only for a verified copy. Read the steps and keep the originals. Do not try to reclaim space during this first test.

## Send feedback

[Open the beta form](https://github.com/Raxuis/volumilo-releases/issues/new?template=beta-feedback.yml) · [Report a bug](https://github.com/Raxuis/volumilo-releases/issues/new?template=bug-report.yml) · [Suggest an idea](https://github.com/Raxuis/volumilo-releases/issues/new?template=feature-request.yml)

**Feedback is public.** Use fictitious names and hide names, paths, accounts and personal information in screenshots. Do not attach personal files, catalogues, transfer journals or analysis folders. These forms do not ask for an email address.

The most useful questions:

- What did you manage to do?
- Where did you hesitate or encounter a problem?
- What would make you keep using the app?

Sending feedback does not authorize its use as a testimonial. Any proposed quotation will need a separate, specific request for permission.

## Known limits

Most tests have been run on the development Apple Silicon Mac and synthetic volumes, with additional manual SD-card checks. A varied Mac fleet and Intel have not been validated. Automated checks do not replace field feedback. Apple Developer ID signing and notarization remain to be arranged. [Other limits](QUICKSTART.en.md#good-to-know).
