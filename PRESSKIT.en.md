# Presenting Volumilo

[Français](PRESSKIT.fr.md) · [Overview](README.en.md)

## One sentence

Volumilo is a free native macOS app for understanding storage, searching saved catalogues of disconnected disks and preparing verified copies to an external drive.

## Short description

Explore what takes up space with a chart and file list. Keep offline catalogues of external volumes you explicitly choose to save. Prepare a copy to another disk, verify its contents, then review the originals separately. Volumilo also offers application storage, guided cleanup and a developer workspace.
